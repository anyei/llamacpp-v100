# #85 gate-read — pricing verification-free emission with the measured #80 legs (2026-08-03)

Inputs: V4 union curve (#84: w2 1.62x / w4 2.65x / w5 3.11x / w8 4.35x member reads),

## The law, now confirmed end-to-end on the fleet

Per-pass cost from the measured legs (100-token greedy, same serve protocol):

- no-spec: 4.19 t/s = 1 token/pass -> ~239 ms/pass
- spec n3: 3.37 t/s at mean accepted len ~2.35/pass -> ~1.43 pass/s -> ~697 ms/pass
- pass-cost ratio 697/239 = **2.9x ~= the w4 union multiplier 2.65 + drafter overhead**

Break-even chain length = the pass-cost ratio (~2.9 tokens). Measured chains: 2.1-2.8
(n3), 1.6-3.0 (n5, saturating - deeper drafts do NOT lengthen accepted chains, they
only widen the union). Prose chains ~2.35 < 2.9 -> ratio 0.80 (the measured 3.37/4.19).
The 2026-08-02 smoke (85% acceptance -> chains ~3) sat right AT break-even: 5.06/4.7 =
1.08. **DSpark on the EP fleet is prompt-class-marginal: >=~85% acceptance content
breaks even (+8%), prose loses 20%, block-5 loses 36%. #84's "2.65 < 2.8 = plausible"
verdict is refined: the drafter+boundary overhead pushes effective break-even to ~2.9
member-read-equivalents - V4 spec is only viable where acceptance chains reach ~3.**

## Pricing the #85 shapes

**(a) CLP-class verification-free emission** (skip verify on high-confidence blocks,
still pay trunk KV catch-up): the catch-up ingest of a k-token accepted block is a
k-lane union forward ~= the (k+1)-lane verify minus one lane. Saving per skipped
verify = the marginal lane only: w4->w5 = 3.11-2.65 = 0.46x of one forward's expert
reads = ~17% of the verify's reads. Even at 100% skip rate the read reduction is
~17%; at a realistic near-lossless skip bucket (purity >=99% - necessarily far below
the 50% raw prose acceptance; the conf head at 0.3 admits ~50% wrong-draft tokens)
the achievable reduction is ~5-8%. **The #84 decision rule (>=20% member-read
reduction at <=1% flips) FAILS with margin -> CLP-class build closes NEGATIVE on V4.**

**(b) confidence-skip of verify entirely (SS-MoE class)**: same marginal-lane
arithmetic, same negative verdict, plus lossy risk grows with task hardness
(2607.26627) and our coherence gates are the only detector - not worth the
quality exposure for <10% reads.

**(c) boundary-chain amortization**: the boundary chain count per emitted token DOES
drop with chain length (1 chain per ~2.35 tokens vs 1 per token) - this is the one
real spec win on the fleet, but it is already priced INTO the measured 0.80/1.08
ratios; there is no separate skip-side saving to add.
